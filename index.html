// the mod

What We're Building

As it's core, we are creating the global modification for Vintage Story which can afford players an opportunity to influence and to increase the available space with their joint efforts. In addition to it, disconnection between them can lead to a shrink of the world for virtual life.

In case of Vintage Story it means:

  • Four different world states from Rust Layer to Freed Layer and to Personal and to Settlement Layers
  • The system we called "Breathing World", which means that players can increase the available space of the world with combined efforts, but also lose the space without cooperation.
  • Civilization Points (CP) system. All positive creating activity will give these points and increase the level of a settlement. Better settlement - more opportunity to increase the space.
  • Safe public place - the Main City where players can rest and buy equipment
  • Additional mods that increase the immersion (environment, world generation, graphic)
