// the mod

What We're Building

The world of Eventide is not static. It breathes — expanding where players cooperate, contracting where they don't. In Vintage Story, that looks like this:

Four world states Rust, Freed, Personal, and Settlement — each unlocking more freedom to build, claim, and interact with the world.
The Breathing World Cooperation pushes the Rust back and expands accessible territory. Civilization Points drop to zero — and it creeps back in.
Civilization Points Every constructive action earns CP. Higher levels protect territory and unlock deeper possibilities — but no one gets there alone.
The Main City A shared safe zone where players can rest and restock.
A curated mod bundle Hand-picked additions for immersion, world generation, and visuals — already built, already tested, ready to play.
on vintage story

Vintage Story is an uncompromising wilderness survival sandbox game inspired by eldritch horror themes. Find yourself in a ruined world reclaimed by nature and permeated by unnerving temporal disturbances. Relive the advent of human civilization, or take your own path.

Source: the official Vintage Story website.
